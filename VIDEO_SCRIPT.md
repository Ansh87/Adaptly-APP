# SATGene: 3-Minute Demo Video Script

General notes: screen-record the live site (https://satgene.netlify.app/) in Demo
Student mode so the data is already realistic, no need to build up history live.
Narrate over your own screen capture; no editing tricks needed, just clean cuts
between the six beats below. Target run time is under 3:00, so keep narration tight
and let the product speak.

---

### Beat 1: The problem (0:00 to 0:25)

**Show:** Login page.

**Say:** "Good SAT tutoring works because a tutor watches a student and constantly
adjusts what to work on. That's expensive, and the students who need it most rarely
get it. SATGene is an agent that does that watching and adjusting automatically, for
free. Let's look at it in action." Click **Try Demo Student**.

### Beat 2: The agent, not a chatbot (0:25 to 1:00)

**Show:** Home page, Next Best Action, Today's Mission, Mastery Snapshot.

**Say:** "This isn't a chatbot you have to prompt. SATGene already looked at this
student's scores, practice, and mistakes and decided what to work on: Advanced Math,
because [read the actual 'Why this?' reason on screen]. That reason comes from a
deterministic engine, not a guess. No AI call happens to make this decision." Point
at the mastery snapshot: "Every skill without direct evidence honestly says 'Not
assessed.' SATGene never invents a mastery score."

### Beat 3: Guided practice (1:00 to 1:50)

**Show:** Click "Start Recommended Practice." Answer a question, get it wrong on
purpose.

**Say:** "The agent launches practice directly on the skill it picked." Click
**Guide Me**: "Instead of just handing over the answer, it walks the student
through it Socratically, one hint at a time." Reveal the answer, click **Explain
differently**: "and can call an AI tutor for a second explanation, with an instant
fallback if that ever fails, so the app never breaks."

### Beat 4: Measurable, automatic adaptation (1:50 to 2:30)

**Show:** Finish the practice set, go to Home again, point at updated Mastery
Snapshot and "SATGene Noticed."

**Say:** "As soon as the student finishes, mastery updates. No button press. Next
time they open the app, SATGene has already re-diagnosed on its own and tells them
exactly what changed and why their plan shifted." Quick cut to **Progress**: "and
the same evidence rolls up into score trends, readiness, and next steps."

### Beat 5: Why it matters for underserved schools (2:30 to 2:50)

**Show:** Quick cuts: Practice → Resources tab (free official links), Login page's
"Try Demo Student" CTA.

**Say:** "Every decision the agent makes runs at zero AI cost, so a school with no
budget still gets a working adaptive tutor. Practice content is either original or
routes to free official resources. And anyone can try the full experience with one
click, no signup."

### Beat 6: Close (2:50 to 3:00)

**Show:** Home page, agent badge visible.

**Say:** "SATGene: it learns from a student's results, decides what to work on,
tutors them through it, and adapts automatically as they improve. Thanks for
watching."
